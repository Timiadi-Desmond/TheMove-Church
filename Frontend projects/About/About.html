About TheMove Church

A community of believers committed to transforming lives through
faith

Our story

“Holy men of God spoke as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.” — 2 Peter 1:21 The word "move"
comes from the Greek "phero," meaning to carry, to bear, to advance while bearing weight, and
to endure through difficulty. This captures our essence. We are not just spreading, we are
carrying the revelation of Jesus, which is truth, light, and love.

We are God’s partners in bearing His message across the nations, advancing His purpose while enduring
the journey.

We recognize the deep need in our world, a hunger for truth, power, and genuine faith. In
response, God has raised The Move as a people commissioned to reveal Jesus, demonstrate His
power, and model what a sound local Church should look like — in doctrine, prayer, consecration,
fervency, love, and in the demonstration of power.

Our Mission

We are building strong discipleship communities
rooted in love, sound doctrine, and the power of the
Spirit. We are raising ministers - men and women -
who will plant and strengthen local churches and ministry expressions globally. We are here to reveal
Jesus, demonstrate His power, and model what the
local Church should look like.

Our Vision

We envision a global family of people, saved and
growing in the knowledge of Christ, until the whole
earth is filled with that knowledge. Simply put: We want
to see lives transformed by Jesus everywhere.
