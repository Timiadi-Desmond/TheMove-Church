We are a community of believers walking daily with God and growing in the knowledge of Christ.

Whether you're new to faith or have been on this journey for years, there's a place for you here. We're here to reveal Jesus, demonstrate His power, and model what the local Church should look like.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

We are building strong discipleship communities rooted in love,
sound doctrine, and the power of the Spirit. We are raising
ministers - men and women - who will plant and strengthen local
churches and ministry expressions globally. We are here to
reveal Jesus, demonstrate His power, and model what the local
Church should look like.

Our Vision

We envision a global family of people, saved and growing in the
knowledge of Christ, until the whole earth is filled with that
knowledge. Simply put: We want to see lives transformed by
Jesus everywhere.
